  How I'd rank Pikachu

  Overall: lower tier — roughly a 4/10 as a raw stat-line. At 320 total it sits
  far below fully-evolved Pokémon (typically 480–540) and legendaries (~680). It
  only clearly beats "joke" pre-evolutions like Magikarp.

  A few specifics:

  - Speed (90) is its one real asset — genuinely good, faster than
  Charizard-tier isn't true (Charizard 100), but 90 outruns a large chunk of the
  roster and lets it strike first.
  - Everything else is weak. HP 35 and Defense 40 make it extremely fragile; a
  single strong hit usually ends it. Its offenses (55 / 50) are mediocre, so
  even its speed doesn't convert into big damage.
  - It's outclassed by its own evolution. Raichu improves every stat — +165
  total. Purely by the numbers, Pikachu is a "should-be-evolved" stage; it stays
  unevolved mostly for mascot reasons, and in-game it can hold the Light Ball
  item that doubles its Attack/Sp. Atk to stay relevant.

  Bottom line: as a standalone stat-line Pikachu ranks near the bottom of the
  pack — a fast but frail glass cannon that's really a stepping stone to Raichu.
  Its fame is cultural, not competitive.
